The scale problem

You asked for 60 × 40 × 5 mm. You got a grain of rice.

Generating CAD code isn't the hard part. Getting the real dimensions right is. Training data lived in a tiny normalized coordinate space, so models learned to write numbers close to zero.

Keep scrolling. Mujassam grows the part to true size and the dimension lines snap into place.

Prompt guide

How to ask for a part.

Clear requests build first time. Vague ones make the robot guess, and guessing is how parts come out the wrong size.

  1. Always give millimetres.Write 60 × 40 × 5 mm, not "small" or "about this big".
  2. Name the part type.Plate, bearing mount, bracket, shaft collar. The name picks the recipe.
  3. List features with their sizes.Hole Ø, how many, and where: 4 holes Ø5 mm, 6 mm from each corner.

Vague vs good

plate with some holes

Plate 60 × 40 × 5 mm with 4 holes Ø5 mm, 6 mm from each corner

a small bracket

U sensor bracket 50 × 30 × 35 mm, 5 mm thick, Ø10 mm sensor hole

collar for my shaft

Split shaft collar, 16 mm bore, 32 mm OD, 12 mm wide

Check your prompt

  • ✓Gives sizes in mm
  • ✓Names the part type
  • ✓Overall size, like W × D × H
  • ✓Feature sizes, like hole Ø
  • ✓How many features, or where
